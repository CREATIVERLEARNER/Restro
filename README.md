# Calcutta Heritage Kitchen & Bistro 🍽️
### *Void-Inspired Minimalist Restaurant Web Experience — Park Street • Kolkata*

An ultra-minimalist, responsive single-page website for an authentic Kolkata restaurant, styled after modern spatial UI design principles (inspired by *void-study.vercel.app*):

- **Palette**: Pure Black (`#000000`), Crisp White (`#ffffff`), Muted Zinc Grays, and **Natural Leaf Green** (`#15803d`).
- **Aesthetic**: **NO glows, NO gradients, NO hue shifts**. Clean, razor-sharp 1px flat borders and solid surfaces.
- **Typography**: Editorial serif (*Newsreader*) + Clean geometric sans (*Inter*).
- **Day & Night Mode Toggle**: Seamless, zero-flash toggle between Night Mode (pitch black `#000000`) and Day Mode (crisp white `#ffffff`). Accessible in the sticky header, mobile drawer, or via `Shift + D`.
- **Heritage System**: Structured historical Bento showcase highlighting the 1856 Metiabruz Awadhi dum origin, 1928 Park Street establishment, 3 culinary pillars (Pillar I: Handi Dum, Pillar II: Granite Ground Masalas, Pillar III: Botanical Infusions), and 4-metric provenance strip.
- **Featured Bento Grid**: 12-column architectural Bento layout with dual-column flagship card, philosophy showcase, and dish cards.
- **Tactile Paper Texture**: Organic Japanese washi/cotton paper grain overlay over food photography.
- **Hero Carousel**: Auto-advancing, seamless crossfading carousel with clean numerical counter (`01 / 04`).
- **Minimalist WhatsApp Actions**: Clean natural green WhatsApp SVG logo icon buttons.
- **In-Browser Visual Admin CMS**: Manage dishes, prices, and settings directly in-browser (`Ctrl + Shift + A`).

---

## 🚀 Running Locally

Open in your browser:
**[http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080)**

- **Toggle Day / Night Mode**: Click the Sun/Moon icon in the top header, or press `Shift + D`.
- **Open In-Browser CMS**: Click `CMS` in the top bar or press `Ctrl + Shift + A`.
- **Heritage System**: Navigate to `#heritage` or click **Heritage** in the top navigation bar.
