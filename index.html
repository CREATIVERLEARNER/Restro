Awadh Meets Bengal (1856 – 1928)

When Nawab Wajid Ali Shah arrived at Metiabruz, his master royal khansamas introduced the sacred art of Persian saffron dum, weaving it together with Bengal's golden potato, virgin mustard oil, and Gondhoraj lime aromatics.

Established on Park Street in 1928, Calcutta Heritage preserves every nuance of that historic harmony without shortcut or dilution.

Pillar I • Awadhi Dum

Dough-Sealed Brass Handis

Slow coal ember cooking inside hand-hammered brass vessels sealed with wheat dough, ensuring zero steam escapes and every grain of basmati absorbs the rich bone marrow essence.

Secret: Wheat Dough Seal • Saffron Marrow Dum
Pillar II • Stone Ground

Granite-Crushed Masalas

Fresh daily stone grinding of shahi jeera, javitri (mace), green cardamom, and cinnamon in granite mortars, mixed with cold-pressed virgin mustard oil and farm desi ghee.

Secret: Shil Nora Mortar • Javitri & Elaichi
Pillar III • Botanical Infusion

Charred Banana Leaves & Lime

Enveloping fresh daily catch in green banana leaves over cast iron griddles, finished with cold-pressed mustard emulsion and the sacred citrus fragrance of royal Gondhoraj lime.

Secret: Cast Iron Griddle • Gondhoraj King Lime
